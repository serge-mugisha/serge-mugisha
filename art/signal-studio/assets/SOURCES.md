# Artwork sources

GitHub mark and link icons derive from [Primer Octicons](https://github.com/primer/octicons), under the repository-root `assets/OCTICONS-LICENSE.txt` MIT license. The official mark-github-24 silhouette was sampled at 16 × 16 pixels. Link, paintbrush, person, and mail icons retain their official vector paths. The person icon represents the LinkedIn profile link.

[JetBrains Mono](https://github.com/JetBrains/JetBrainsMono) Regular and Bold are bundled under `OFL-JetBrainsMono.txt`. GSAP retains its distribution license header.

The code and image-thumbnail tile drawings, music notes, and contribution grid are original. Pac-Man is a visual homage to the classic arcade character. The grid makes no claim about contribution counts.

The treble (G) clef pixel template samples the musical symbol U+1D11E from [Noto Music](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/notomusic), under `OFL-NotoMusic.txt`. Its 11 × 26 cell template is embedded in the source; the font is not required at runtime.

The walking builder sprite and hammer are original pixel artwork.
